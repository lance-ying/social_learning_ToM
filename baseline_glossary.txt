Baseline Glossary

social_mentalizing
- Observes other agents and updates which blue wizard is plausible from their behavior.
- Uses social observation to narrow the hidden blue-wizard possibilities before agent1 plans.

social_mentalizing_until_one_converges
- Same idea as social_mentalizing, but stops observing once one observed agent has converged to a single relevant blue-wizard candidate.
- In exp1 and exp2, plotting aliases this to social_mentalizing because the variant does not exist there.

rational_non_mentalizing
- Observes other agents but does not run a full social-mentalizing posterior model.
- In exp4 reconstruction, observed wizard choices are used to prioritize agent1's own greedy wizard search rather than latent-world replay.

naive_observer
- Watches other agents with a simple observation policy, then agent1 follows a simple self-search policy.
- In exp4 reconstruction, observed wizard choices are used to prioritize greedy search order.

agent1_naive_planner
- Non-observing planner.
- Agent1 ignores social observations and greedily searches blue wizards itself until it gets the blue amulet, then heads to the goal.

rational_non_mentalizing_expert_only_until_expert_wizard
- Exp4 variant.
- Observes only the expert agent until the expert reaches its first wizard interaction.
- In reconstruction, agent1 tries the expert's observed wizard first, then greedy-searches the remaining blue wizards.

rational_non_mentalizing_novice_full_expert_until_expert_wizard
- Exp4 variant.
- Observes the full novice trajectory and observes the expert until the expert reaches its first wizard interaction.
- In reconstruction, agent1 tries the expert's observed wizard first, then the novice's last observed wizard, then greedy-searches the remaining blue wizards.

naive_observer_expert_only_until_expert_wizard
- Exp4 variant.
- Same observation schedule as the rational variant above, but paired with the naive observer family.
- In reconstruction, agent1 tries the expert's observed wizard first, then greedy-searches the remaining blue wizards.

naive_observer_novice_full_expert_until_expert_wizard
- Exp4 variant.
- Same observation schedule as the rational variant above, but paired with the naive observer family.
- In reconstruction, agent1 tries the expert's observed wizard first, then the novice's last observed wizard, then greedy-searches the remaining blue wizards.
